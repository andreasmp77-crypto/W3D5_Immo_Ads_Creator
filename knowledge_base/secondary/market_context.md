# Berlin Market Context & Terminology

## Local Housing Terms & Nuances
- **Altbau:** Turn-of-the-century building (pre-WWII), typically featuring high ceilings (3m+), hardwood floors (*Dielen* or *Parkett*), and decorative stucco (*Stuck*).
- **Neubau:** Modern construction (post-1990 or contemporary), emphasizing energy efficiency, underground parking, elevator access, and open-plan layouts.
- **EBK (Einbauküche):** Fitted kitchen included with the property.
- **Balkon / Loggia / Dachterrasse:** Outdoor balcony, recessed loggia, or rooftop terrace—highly prized selling points in Berlin.

## Key Local Selling Highlights
- **Public Transport Proximity:** In Berlin, being within walking distance (less than 10 minutes or 800m) to a U-Bahn, S-Bahn, or Tram stop is a major selling point.
- **Family Infrastructure:** High density of Kitas (daycares) and primary schools within the immediate ZIP code strongly enhances family appeal.
- **Green Spaces:** Proximity to major public parks (e.g., Tiergarten, Volkspark Friedrichshain, Tempelhofer Feld) adds significant lifestyle value.
